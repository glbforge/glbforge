Privacy Policy

Effective: August 20, 2026

The short version: the studio's analysis, optimization, forging, and STL tools run entirely in your browser — those files never leave your device. The only data that touches our servers is what's needed for sign-in, credits, and the images you explicitly submit for AI generation.

What we collect, and why

DataWhyWhere it lives
OAuth identity (GitHub/Google user id, username or email)Your account and sign-inCloudflare (our database)
Credit balance and purchase recordsOperating the prepaid credit systemCloudflare; payment details stay with Stripe
Generation task records (task ids, model used, timestamps)Delivering your results and your generation historyCloudflare
Images you submit for generationPassed to the AI provider you chose to produce your modelTransiently on our worker; processed by Meshy or fal.ai per their policies

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, no sale of personal data.
  • No tracking cookies — we set one sign-in session cookie and one short-lived cookie during the OAuth redirect. That's all.
  • Files you drop into the in-browser studio are stored only in your own browser (IndexedDB) so they survive reloads; we cannot see them.
  • We do not use your uploads or generations to train models.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe; we receive confirmation of payment and the pack purchased, never your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Retention & deletion

Account, credit, and task records are kept while your account exists. Email support@glbforge.dev to delete your account and its data; unused paid credits will be refunded per the refund policy.

Changes & contact

Material changes will be dated at the top of this page. Questions: support@glbforge.dev.
